### Q02: How did the relief rate for credit card complaints change each year from 2022 to 2025?

- **Expected:** 2022=31.9%; 2023=36.5%; 2024=39.0%; 2025=33.3%
- **What was wrong:** missing 33.3% for 2025
- **Tools called:** query_metrics

Answer:

> Credit card relief rates rose from 31.9% in 2022 to a peak of 39.0% in 2024, then fell to 33.2% in 2025.
>
> | Year received | Relief rate | Complaints |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 2022 | 31.9% | 36,582 |
> | 2023 | 36.5% | 54,413 |
> | 2024 | 39.0% | 75,989 |
> | 2025 | 33.2% | 89,978 |
>
> The period is complaints received from 1 January 2022 to 31 December 2025, filtered to the Credit card product group, which leaves out prepaid cards. The metric is the relief rate: the share of closed complaints with a known outcome in which the consumer got relief, monetary or not. Complaint counts are all complaints received in each year, and the four years together total 256,962 complaints. No company or other filters were applied.
>
> Warnings from the tools:
> - 2011 covers December only and 2026 runs to 31 August; compare whole years, or the same months of each year.
> - Relief rates count closed complaints with a known outcome only; the old "Closed" and "Untimely response" values say nothing about relief and are left out.
> - Nearly 10% of 2026 complaints were still in progress when the archive was published, so outcomes for recent months are incomplete.
> - Complaints are unverified consumer reports: a count shows what was reported, not what happened.
> - Credit reporting makes up about 81% of all complaints, so totals across products mostly reflect credit reporting.
